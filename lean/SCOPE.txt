====================================================================================================
SCOPE of the formalisation: the standard, the form of the statements, and what is outside
====================================================================================================

------------------------------------------- The standard -------------------------------------------

The library CoSimFoundations is written in Lean 4, toolchain leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0
(file lean-toolchain), with Mathlib at the tag v4.35.0, commit
56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679 (file lake-manifest.json). It has 101 modules in the folder
CoSimFoundations, with 1,654 theorems and 214 definitions and structures in 31,451 lines; the root
file CoSimFoundations.lean imports all of them. The library imports Mathlib and nothing else: every
import line of the 101 modules, of the root file and of the audit file names a module of Mathlib or
of the library itself. It contains no unproved step and declares no axiom: a search of these 103
files for the words sorry, admit, native_decide, axiom, unsafe, opaque, implemented_by, extern and
set_option, as whole words in code and comments, finds none of them. The library is compiled with
autoImplicit switched off and with warnings treated as errors (lakefile.toml), so that a declaration
that uses sorry would stop the build.

The audit. CoSimFoundationsAudit.lean imports the library and consists of 1,649 lines of the form
"#print axioms CoSimFoundations.name", one for each public theorem by its fully qualified name. When
the file is built, Lean prints for each of them the axioms on which its proof depends, through all
lemmas that it uses. The standard is met if the build ends without an error and every printed list
contains only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, the three standard axioms of Lean. The five
theorems that the audit does not list are private lemmas, which cannot be named outside their
module; the text search above covers them as well.

To build, in this folder: "lake exe cache get" downloads the compiled Mathlib, and "lake build" then
builds the two default targets, the library and the audit. A Lean process that imports Mathlib needs
several gigabytes of memory, and lake runs several at once; on a machine with little memory the
modules can be built one at a time, for example "lake build CoSimFoundations.Setting", and the audit
last with "lake build CoSimFoundationsAudit".

------------------------------------ The form of the statements ------------------------------------

The statements are stated for operators on real normed or inner product spaces, or for real matrices
with a block pattern (the structure HeldScheme: the matrices F, G^new C, G^old C and X on states
that a map groups into subsystems, with a partner relation and stages). They do not assume
power-conjugate ports unless the statement of the paper does.

Statements that the paper makes to leading order, with a remainder O(H^p), are formalised at one
macro step with an explicit bound on the remainder, for every scheme whose ||G^new C|| and ||X - I||
are bounded by given multiples a H and b H and whose ||X - U|| is bounded by a given multiple c H^2.
Nothing is assumed of a family of schemes, and the constants of the remainders are explicit
functions of a, b, c and ||A||. The zero-order hold is proved to satisfy the three bounds for
H ||A|| <= 1/4 and s H ||A|| <= 1/6, with s the square root of the number of subsystems and
constants in ||A|| and s. For example, the zero-order hold has ||Delta - (1/2) H A_sig
A|| <= (37 + 220 s + 53 s^2 + 54 s^3) ||A||^3 H^2 for 0 < H, H ||A|| <= 1/24 and s H ||A|| <= 1/6.
The modified system is the logarithm series of X divided by H; it exists for ||X - I|| < 1, and the
statements about it are formalised for ||X - I|| <= 1/3 and H ||A|| <= 1/4.

The following hypotheses, which the paper leaves to its standing assumptions, are explicit in the
Lean statements: an invertible phi in (10) and wherever Psi_0, E or Q occur
(proved for H ||A_0|| <= 1); H != 0 or H > 0 where a statement divides by H; integer offsets in
(16); surjective outputs of the partners, given by right inverses, in (23); in Theorem 4.4 (i), in
Theorem 4.5 (i) and in (42) a bounded right inverse of the stacked maps, on which the constants
depend, with H ||A_i|| <= 1 and a smallness condition on H times the norm of that inverse, and
H <= 1 in the last two; in Theorem 4.5 (ii) the constant 2 b of Lemma A.1 in the place of 5.3;
0 <= H and l >= 1 in Lemma A.1; distinct stages for the determinant of Proposition 5.1; B and L
given as data in the form of (31) at the ports, which holds for every interconnection L; that a
subsystem is not its own partner in the statements on a diagonal block. The injection rate and the
added damping enter as arbitrary bounds of the quadratic form of Delta_R and not as eigenvalues. The
largest relative error of Theorem 4.5 and Lemma A.1 enters as an arbitrary upper bound of the
relative errors of all runs. The asymptotic part of Corollary 3.4 is stated as a condition on the
eigenvalues.

-------------------------------- What is outside the formalisation ---------------------------------

CORRESPONDENCE.txt gives the status of every numbered statement and equation. Of the numbered
statements and their parts, the following are not formalised, or are formalised in a special case
only.

  - Section 2. In (1) and (2): the subsystems with their matrices B_i, C_j as objects and the block
    structure A_ij = B_i L_ij C_j; the library takes a matrix A with the block pattern. In
    Definition 2.1 (i) to (iii): weights that depend on the local time, and the derivation of the
    coefficients from the differential equations for subsystems with several states. In Definition
    2.2: the condition on the eigenvalues, that the logarithm series is the principal logarithm, and
    the uniqueness of the modified system. In (8): that sym A is block diagonal, as a declaration.
    Example 2.3.
  - Section 3. In Lemma 3.1: the closed form of Psi_0 with its expansion, and (11) as a differential
    equation. In Remark 3.2: the second order of the alternating scheme in its numerical
    dissipation, and its identification with the Strang splitting. Remark 3.3 with (14). In
    Corollary 3.4: the part on stability that is not asymptotic, and the Jordan chains, since the
    library has no Jordan form. In Corollary 3.5: that the eigenvalues of the modified system are
    even in H.
  - Section 4. In Theorem 4.2: the identity T_i v = Pi_i U v on the states not shown as a
    declaration of its own (its consequence for the norm is formalised). (24) for subsystems with
    more than one state. Example 4.3. (26) on the states that a subsystem is shown. In Theorem 4.4
    (ii): the printed form, which follows from the formalised one by a division. In Theorem 4.5: the
    last sentence, on one held sample, as one statement.
  - Section 5. In Proposition 5.1: the determinant, and nu = 0, when two subsystems that are not
    partners share a stage. In Lemma 5.2: that equality holds in (34) for one pair, and that a pair
    in the state spaces of two subsystems attains the norm of the block. In Corollary 5.4 (ii): the
    characterisation by proper interval graphs, which rests on a cited theorem of graph theory; the
    path executed from end to end is formalised. In Proposition 5.5: Delta_R = 0 and that the
    difference is a numerical interconnection, for ||X - I|| > 1/3. Example 5.6. In Proposition 5.7:
    the size O(H^3 |x^k|^2) of the term with skew Psi_0, its vanishing when some A_i are symmetric
    and others skew, and that the second term of (37) is absent only if no two partners share a
    stage.
  - Section 6. In Proposition 6.1: the two inequalities that follow (38). Example 6.2, Example 6.3.

Statements of the running text have a row in CORRESPONDENCE.txt where the library has a declaration
for them. Of the running text, the following are outside the formalisation.

  - Section 2.2: the scheme of the class that holds a sample with feedthrough of a partner; that a
    local gain realises every F_i > 0 and every G_ij for a scalar store in an arbitrary order of
    execution (formalised for a Jacobi scheme and for one sequential order).
  - Section 2.3: that a modified system exists only if X has no eigenvalue on the half line from
    minus infinity to 0, and that Delta = 0 only if X = U; the modified system of a scheme with
    several held samples and its parasitic eigenvalues.
  - Section 2.4: the derivative of the energy along the modified system; for a lossless system, that
    conservation of the energy implies Delta_R = 0 when ||X - I|| > 1/3; H nu = -ln det D, and the
    leading values of s_+ and s_-.
  - Section 3.1: the relation between the pair and the modified system through the hyperbolic
    tangent, and Ehat^(-1) Ahat = At + O(H^2); the values of H for which Psi_0 is defined.
  - Section 3.2: the macro step at which a mode of the Jacobi hold loses its damping; the frequency
    of the leapfrog method; that a link within a stage and a cycle with unequal numbers of links to
    a later and to an earlier stage have no offsets.
  - Section 4.1 and Section 4.2: the formula for f_i and the bound on c_i for scalar stores; the
    exact rule with two held samples on the lossless chain of three; the rule of fourth order from
    two held samples of an output and of its rate.
  - Section 5.1: that the block of sym(A_sig A) between two partners vanishes between the states
    that the two outputs do not show (the cancellation is formalised with the coefficient row as a
    hypothesis).
  - Section 5.2: that the largest entry of Delta_R between two orthogonal states attains half the
    spread mu + iota; tau_k = (p_i - 2 p_k + p_j)/2 with offsets; that A_ik A_kj vanishes if the two
    ports of the common partner act in orthogonal directions.
  - Section 5.3: the star with three leaves and the ring; the damped sweep as a splitting.
  - Section 5.4: x^T A_seq x at the ports; the modified Hamiltonian of the symplectic Euler method
    for two stores; N + N^T = H A_seq for the zero-order hold, which the bound (1 + rho)/(1 - rho)
    on the energy needs; the threshold H lambda_max(A_seq) = 2; the statement on coupling graphs
    with a cycle.
  - Section 7.1: that a Jacobi scheme with the first-order input has X - U = O(H^3).
  - Appendix A.3: skew Psi_0 = (1/12) H^2 skew(A_0^2) + O(H^4).

Example 2.3, Example 4.3, Example 5.6, Example 6.2, Example 6.3, the numbers of the two tables and
the numbers of the figures are computed, not proved; the code that computes them is published with
the library.
