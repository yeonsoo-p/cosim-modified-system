====================================================================================================
CORRESPONDENCE between the statements of the paper and the Lean library CoSimFoundations
====================================================================================================

This file gives, for every numbered statement of the paper (definitions, lemmas, theorems,
corollaries, propositions, remarks, examples) and for every numbered equation, the Lean declarations
that formalise it and a status. The rows stand in the order of the paper. A statement with several
parts has one row for each part. Statements of the running text have a row where the library has a
declaration for them; they carry the label "(text)". The numbers are those printed in the paper; the
labels are those of its LaTeX source, by which the headers of the Lean modules name the statements.
The standard of the formalisation and the list of what is outside it are in SCOPE.txt.

Lean names are relative to the namespace CoSimFoundations and are followed by nothing if they are
theorems of the audit list CoSimFoundationsAudit.lean, by "(def)" if they are definitions or
structures (the audit lists theorems only), and by "(+)" if they are theorems that the audit does
not list. Each name is preceded by the file of its module in the folder CoSimFoundations. Formulas
are written in plain text: A_0 = diag(A_i) and A_c are the block-diagonal and the coupling part of
A, A_sig the signed coupling with the blocks sig_ik A_ik, sig_ik = +1 if k runs at an earlier stage
than i and -1 otherwise, sigma_ik = (sig_ik + 1)/2, U = exp(H A), At the modified system, Delta = At
- A, Delta_R and Delta_J its numerical dissipation and interconnection, Ahat and Ehat the pair of a
scheme, T_i the local defect, |.| the Euclidean and ||.|| the spectral norm.

------------------------------------------ Status values -------------------------------------------

  FULL     the Lean statement is the statement of the paper, possibly more general or with
           explicit constants in place of a remainder O(H^p)
  PARTIAL  a stated part is formalised and a stated part is not; the note says which
  SPECIAL  formalised in a special case only; the note says which
  NONE     no Lean statement ("related" then names declarations close to it, if there are any)

------------------------------- Conventions that apply to many rows --------------------------------

  (C1) A statement "to leading order", "+ O(H^p)" or "for small macro steps" is in Lean one scheme
       at one macro step H with an explicit constant in the remainder. The structure
       HeldScheme.Consistent A U H a b c requires H >= 0, U = exp(H A), a matrix A without a block
       between different subsystems that are not partners, and the three bounds ||G^new C|| <= a H,
       ||X - I|| <= b H, ||X - U|| <= c H^2. Neither F = I + O(H) nor a bound on G^old C is assumed.
       The zero-order hold satisfies the three bounds with a = 3 s ||A||, b = 8 ||A||, c = 3
       (1 + 6 s) ||A||^2, s the square root of the number of subsystems, for H ||A|| <= 1/4 and s
       H ||A|| <= 1/6 (HeldScheme.zoh_consistent in ZeroOrderHold.lean).
  (C2) A scheme with one held sample is the structure HeldScheme: matrices F, N = G^new C, M = G^old
       C and X on the states Fin n with X = F + M + N X, a map sub from the states to the
       subsystems, a partner relation and stages; F vanishes between different subsystems, N has
       entries only from partners at an earlier stage, M only from partners that do not run at an
       earlier stage. The objects B_i, L_ij, C_j, G_ij of the paper do not occur unless a row names
       them. A block of a matrix T is expressed by the numbers <x, T y> with x supported on one
       subsystem and y on another, or by the padded blocks HeldScheme.cblock and HeldScheme.blk.
  (C3) The modified system is modifiedSystem H X = (1/H) log X with the logarithm series of Mathlib;
       exp(H At) = X is proved for ||X - I|| < 1. Statements about the modified system of a
       consistent scheme take H > 0, b H <= 1/3 and H ||A|| <= 1/4; for the zero-order hold these
       are H ||A|| <= 1/24 and s H ||A|| <= 1/6.
  (C4) Power-conjugate ports are the hypothesis hskew: A q p = -A p q for states p, q of different
       subsystems. It occurs only where the paper assumes power-conjugate ports. "Partner weights
       only" is F = exp(H A_0), or, for one subsystem s, that the block s of F is that of
       exp(H A_s).
  (C5) The total numerical damping nu is numDamping A H X = -tr Delta. The injection rate iota and
       the added damping mu are not defined as eigenvalues: they enter as any real numbers with
       -iota |v|^2 <= <v, Delta_R v> <= mu |v|^2 for all v, of which -lambda_min and lambda_max are
       the least.
  (C6) Psi_0 is HeldScheme.midpointPsi = phi^(-1) + (H/2) A_0 with phi = phi_1(H A_0) and
       phi_1(Z) = sum_j Z^j/(j+1)!, the matrix diag(phi_i) of the paper. Statements with Psi_0, E or
       Q take the invertibility of phi as a hypothesis; it is proved for H ||A_0|| <= 1
       (isUnit_det_phiOne in MidpointForm.lean).
  (C7) Statements on schemes with l held samples and on what a subsystem is shown are stated for
       operators on real normed spaces: a map P of norm at most one onto the states of subsystem i,
       the coupling row P A = A_i P + sum_j L_j C_j over the partners j with the output maps C_j,
       and sigma_j = 0 or 1 for each partner. The largest relative error eps_K over a run is
       replaced by an arbitrary upper bound delta of the relative errors of all runs; the forms for
       the least upper bound and for single runs are separate declarations where a row names them.
  (C8) "Sequential scheme" (no two partners share a stage) is the hypothesis hns; "distinct stages"
       (no two subsystems share a stage) is the hypothesis hstage.

  MatrixODE              Variation of constants for matrices.
  NoInjection            A one-step map with ||D|| <= 1 that passes nothing between two orthonormal
                         directions: (det D)^2 <= 1 + alpha^2 - 2 (|u| - alpha eta), with u what the
                         monolithic step passes between them.
  NoInjectionJacobi      The statements of TradeOff and NoInjection for Jacobi schemes of scalar
                         stores and two stores that are not partners; for every operator with
                         ||D|| <= 1, (det D)^2 <= 1 - 2 |<D e_1, D e_2>|.
  PairDamping            One macro step of a scheme that passes nothing between two orthonormal
                         directions: the total damping against the gain of energy, in determinant
                         form.
  PairTrace              2 ||T_ij|| <= tr T_ii + tr T_jj + (n_i + n_j) iota <= tr T + n iota for a
                         symmetric matrix T with -iota |x|^2 <= x^T T x and two subsystems i != j
                         with n_i and n_j states.
  Parity                 The sign rule (A^d)_ki = (-1)^d (A^d)_ik for stores d links apart in a
                         network with A_ji = -A_ij, and its consequences for l held samples in a
                         Jacobi scheme.
  RegionTwo              Two states: det(x -> z x + w conj(x)) = |z|^2 - |w|^2, and the bounds of
                         Region are attained.
  RowCoords              Real 2 x 2 maps as x -> z x + w conj(x) on the complex numbers: the largest
                         stretch |z| + |w| and the least stretch ||z| - |w||.
  SpectralSplit          The splitting of a finite-dimensional real space into the invariant
                         subspaces of a linear map that belong to its negative eigenvalues, to the
                         eigenvalue 0 and to its other eigenvalues, with uniqueness.
  Threshold              Two stores: exp(H A) in angle form, and the macro steps up to which a
                         Jacobi scheme or a sequential scheme of two scalar stores can have exactly
                         the monolithic step.
  TwoStoreStep           exp(t B) = exp(c t)(cos(nu t) I + (sin(nu t)/nu) N) for B = c I + N with
                         N^2 = -nu^2 I; the monolithic step of two stores.
